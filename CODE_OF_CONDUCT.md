# KAYVRA Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

KAYVRA aims to maintain a professional, respectful, inclusive, and security-conscious environment across its repositories, discussions, Issues, Pull Requests, and other project spaces.

Participation is expected to be constructive and considerate.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with respect, regardless of experience, background, role, or viewpoint.
- Communicate professionally and critique ideas or code rather than attacking individuals.
- Give useful, specific, and respectful feedback.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and agreed project boundaries.
- Credit the work and contributions of others.
- Share only information you are authorized to disclose.
- Help keep technical discussions focused, safe, and welcoming to good-faith contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:
- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech, sexual harassment, or deliberately humiliating conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private client information, or confidential investigation material.
- Misrepresenting authorization, findings, authorship, or project status.
- Encouraging or conducting unauthorized access, testing, disruption, or data extraction.
- Repeated disruptive behavior or deliberate interference with project work.

## Scope

This Code applies to participation in KAYVRA-managed GitHub spaces and to situations where someone is representing a KAYVRA project or team.

## Reporting a Concern

Report conduct concerns privately to the relevant repository or organization maintainers through an established private communication channel. Include enough context to understand the concern, but do not publish sensitive personal or client information.

Do not use public Issues to disclose security vulnerabilities. Follow the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect people, project integrity, or confidential information.

Reports will be reviewed with care and handled as privately as reasonably possible. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions where doing so is appropriate and safe.

## A Shared Responsibility

Every participant helps maintain a professional environment. Thank you for contributing responsibly.
